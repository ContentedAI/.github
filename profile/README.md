# Contented

Take your team's conversations further.

Contented records meetings and calls, then turns them into the documents
you would otherwise write up afterwards: minutes, an action table, a
decision log, a risk register, a SWOT.

Nothing joins your call. Recording happens on your own phone or computer,
so it works with Microsoft Teams, Zoom and Google Meet, and it works just
as well for a workshop, a site visit or a client session across a table.

## How it works

A conversation is whatever you bring in. Record in the workspace, record
on your phone, upload an audio or video file (MP4, M4A or MP3 up to
500 MB) or upload a transcript you already have. Contented transcribes it
and summarises it without being asked.

Templates are where the work happens. There are more than 50 of them,
written around how a given piece of thinking is normally done, and they
are grouped into modules: Meeting, Marketing and Human Centered, which
covers user research. Pick one and Contented produces the output: a concise or
detailed summary, an action table you can pull down as CSV, a deep dive
on one topic, a SWOT, a risk matrix, a decision log, a key metrics
breakdown. Outputs download as Word or PDF, or copy straight out.

Accuracy improves with a little context. Name the speakers, add attendees
with their job titles and pronouns, and correct the entities Contented
picked up such as company names and place names. Every one of those steps
feeds the next output you generate.

## Privacy

Contented is SOC 2 Type 1 compliant. Models are never trained on customer
data and recordings are not kept inside the apps.

Each conversation carries its own visibility. Share it to the workspace or
keep it to yourself. Sensitive details can be protected with the PII
controls, sign-in is passwordless, and admins get their own dashboard for
managing the workspace.

## Where it runs

The web workspace, a Windows desktop app, iPhone and Android, and inside
Microsoft Teams.
